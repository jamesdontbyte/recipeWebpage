Filipino Style Spaghetti

Filipino-style spaghetti is a sweet and savory pasta dish that differs from traditional Italian spaghetti. It is made with a tomato-based sauce that includes banana ketchup and sugar, giving it a distinctly sweet flavor loved by many Filipinos.

This dish is often served at birthdays and family gatherings and is usually topped with hotdogs and grated cheese. Its unique taste makes it a comfort food for many households in the Philippines.

Ingredients

  • 250 g spaghetti pasta
  • 2 tablespoons cooking oil
  • 3 hotdogs
  • 1 small onion
  • 3 cloves garlic
  • 1 cup tomato sauce
  • 1/2 cup banana ketchup
  • 2 tablespoons sugar
  • Salt and pepper
  • Grated cheese

Steps to make Filipino-Style Spaghetti

  1. Cook the spaghetti pasta according to package instructions, then set aside.
  2. Heat oil in a pan and sauté garlic and onion until fragrant.
  3. Add the sliced hotdogs and cook for a few minutes.
  4. Pour in the tomato sauce and banana ketchup, then stir well.
  5. Add sugar, salt, and pepper, and let the sauce simmer.
  6. Add the cooked spaghetti to the sauce and mix until evenly coated.
  7. Serve hot and top with grated cheese.
