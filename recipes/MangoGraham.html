Mango Grahams

Prep: 20 min | Cook: 0 min Difficulty: Easy

Mango Graham Float is a popular Filipino no‑bake dessert made by layering graham crackers, sweetened cream, and ripe mangoes. It is commonly served during gatherings and celebrations because it is easy to prepare and does not require an oven.

This dessert is loved for its creamy texture and the natural sweetness of ripe mangoes. After chilling, the graham crackers soften and absorb the cream, creating a cake‑like consistency that is both refreshing and satisfying.

Ingredients

  • 2–3 ripe mangoes
  • 1 pack graham crackers
  • 2 cups all‑purpose cream
  • 1/2 cup condensed milk

Steps to make Mango Grahams

  • In a bowl, mix the all‑purpose cream and condensed milk until well combined.
  • In a dish, arrange a layer of graham crackers at the bottom.
  • Spread a layer of cream mixture over the crackers.
  • Add a layer of sliced mangoes on top of the cream.
  • Repeat the layers until all ingredients are used.
  • Finish with cream and mangoes on top.
  • Refrigerate for at least 4 hours or overnight before serving.
