Chicken Cordon Bleu

Chicken Cordon Bleu is a classic dish made from chicken breast that is filled with ham and cheese, then rolled, breaded, and cooked until golden brown. The chicken is usually pounded thin so it can be easily wrapped around the filling, which allows the cheese to melt inside while keeping the outside crispy. Swiss cheese is traditionally used, but other mild, melty cheeses can also work well.

This recipe is known for its balance of textures and flavors: the crunchy breadcrumb coating on the outside, the juicy chicken, and the savory, creamy filling inside. Chicken Cordon Bleu is often served with simple side dishes such as rice, mashed potatoes, or vegetables, making it a popular choice for both home cooking and special occasions.

Ingredients

  • 2 boneless, skinless chicken breasts
  • Salt and pepper
  • 4 slices of ham
  • 4 slices of Swiss cheese
  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1 large egg
  • 1 cup breadcrumbs
  • 2 tablespoons cooking oil or butter

Steps to make Chicken Cordon Bleu

  1. Preheat the oven to 180 °C (350 °F) or prepare a pan for frying.
  2. Place the chicken breasts between plastic wrap and gently pound them until they are evenly thin.
  3. Season both sides of the chicken with salt and pepper.
  4. Lay a slice of ham and a slice of cheese on each chicken breast.
  5. Carefully roll the chicken tightly and secure it with toothpicks if needed.
  6. Coat each rolled chicken first in flour, then dip it into the beaten egg, and finally cover it with breadcrumbs.
  7. Heat oil or butter in a pan and cook the chicken until golden brown on all sides, or place it on a baking tray if baking.
  8. Transfer to the oven and cook until the chicken is fully cooked and the cheese inside is melted.
  9. Remove toothpicks before serving and let the chicken rest for a few minutes.
